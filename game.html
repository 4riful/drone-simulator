Flight Manual

Help

Modes

Single and Mission enable combat objectives. Training removes pressure. Free Flight disables enemies. Online Battle uses Supabase Realtime for same-room battle contacts.

Profiles

Create your own callsign before launch. The HUD, radio calls, battle room identity, local records, and mission insight use that name.

Radio

Radio traffic now addresses your active callsign instead of a fixed default. In online battle, your callsign is made unique for same-room combat.

City Map

The current city uses a generated harbor grid with river/canal roads, bridge crossings, named districts, service lots, landmarks, parks, traffic, and building collision. A custom map can replace the layout data next.

Controls

W/S pitch, A/D roll, Q/E yaw, Space climb, Shift descend, F fire, Tab boost, B or Ctrl brake, T lock, L follow, H help, Esc pause.
