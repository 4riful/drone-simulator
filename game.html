Flight Manual

Help

Modes

Single and Mission enable combat objectives. Training removes pressure. Free Flight disables enemies. Online Battle uses free Supabase Realtime presence for two-player battle rooms.

Profiles

Profiles store local sorties, score, flight time, preferred persona, and selected mode in this browser.

Flight Logic

The current controller is assisted. Treat speed, altitude, link, GPS, battery, wind, and air density as operational limits. A force/torque rewrite is documented in the repo.

Controls

W/S pitch, A/D roll, Q/E yaw, Space climb, Shift descend, F fire, Tab boost, B or Ctrl brake, T lock, L follow, H help, Esc pause.
